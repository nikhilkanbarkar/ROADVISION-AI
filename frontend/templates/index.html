How to Capture Defects

Ensure safe standing on the pavement. Take photos at a 45-degree angle from 2-4 meters away. The AI detects potholes (D40), alligator cracks (D20), and longitudinal cracks (D00).

Repair SLA Timelines

Under municipal citizen charters, Critical Risk potholes on highways are inspected within 24 hours. Arterial and local street potholes are queued for cold-mix bitumen patching within 3 to 5 business days.
